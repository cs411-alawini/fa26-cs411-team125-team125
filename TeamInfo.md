## Project Summary

FareWise is a flight booking time web application that can help users understand historical air travel patterns for specific routes. Users can be able to select origin airports, destination airports, and travel preferences such as weekdays, weekends, and the holiday season. The application will be able to access historical fare data and display how ticket prices change based on how many days before the departure date. This will allow people to see how air travel typically behaves, allowing users to plan their trip on their own terms while saving money along the way. Fairwise will present this information through route-specific price trends based on the user input and will take into consideration booking windows and historical fee comparisons. There will also be a comparison feature to help compare different time ranges with each other, seeing which best suits their travel preferences. Users will be able to support filtering through factors such as airline, departure day, and type of flight(nonstop, connecting, or international). By combining route information with historical pricing data, this application will give users a more detailed view of their plans and show how prices will vary across different conditions, making it easier to have a smoother booking experience for a trip.
